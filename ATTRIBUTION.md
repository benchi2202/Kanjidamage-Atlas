The build fetches the public dataset and a small number of site pages once, caches them locally, and
does not scrape the site at runtime.

- Project: https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/

KanjiVG's hand-annotated SVG data is used for two things:

1. extracting labeled structural components for the KanjiVG + Jaccard lens;
2. rendering normalized 64×64 bitmaps for the pixel-difference lens.
